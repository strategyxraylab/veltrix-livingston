HELPFUL INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions.

What services does VELTRIX LIVINGSTON explore?

Our design focus includes residential interiors, commercial spaces, space styling and interior architecture. The right scope depends on your space and objectives.

How do I start a project?

Contact us with a short description of your space, what you would like to achieve and any inspiration you have. We can then discuss suitable next steps.

Do you work on residential and commercial spaces?

Yes. Our design direction includes homes, workspaces, retail environments and hospitality spaces.

Can I request help with one room?

You can enquire about a single room or a wider interior project. The proposed scope can be discussed before any commitment.

What should I prepare before a consultation?

Photographs, approximate room dimensions, your priorities, preferred style references and an indicative budget can help make the conversation more useful.

Are the images in the gallery completed projects?

The current gallery contains illustrative design inspiration and conceptual references. They should not be interpreted as a portfolio of completed VELTRIX LIVINGSTON commissions.

How can I contact the studio?

You can call 0716397341 or start a conversation on WhatsApp using the contact links on this website.
